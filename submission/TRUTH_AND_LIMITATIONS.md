# Truth and Limitations

1. Current live provider coverage is four verified cells in `phx-downtown`.
2. Phoenix-wide OSM context is not Phoenix-wide thermal observation. Outside the live AOI is “not currently observed.”
3. Intervention cooling effects are modeled planning assumptions, not proven causal outcomes.
4. TEU is a planning exposure metric. VA-TEU is vulnerability-adjusted and may exceed TEU.
5. ThermalWay TEC is modeled cost, not medical risk.
6. Driver attribution is diagnostic, not causal proof.
7. Provider-derived values are never overwritten by synthetic values.
8. CP-SAT finds the best portfolio under its encoded candidates, assumptions, objective, and constraints; this is not a guarantee of implementation success.
9. Gemma explains verified evidence. A fallback is visibly a fallback and must not be described as model-backed success.
10. Human review is required for every recommendation. Field pilots, community review, permitting, maintenance planning, and causal evaluation remain necessary before deployment claims.
