# Judging Q&A

## What is novel?
HELIOS connects observation to governed intervention: exposure, counterfactual design, constrained investment, mobility, provenance, and local explanation in one truth-typed loop.

## Why not just FortyGuard?
FortyGuard supplies authoritative thermal observations. HELIOS preserves those metrics and adds the decision layer: who is exposed, what could be changed, which portfolio fits a budget, how journeys can reduce modeled exposure, and why a recommendation is reviewable.

## Why Gemma?
Local Gemma turns verified evidence into concise decision language without sending civic decision packets to a hosted LLM. It never owns calculations; ContextForge and the firewall constrain its claims.

## Why an optimizer?
Cities face budgets and mutually competing actions. CP-SAT makes portfolio selection reproducible, budget-feasible, and auditable instead of relying on narrative preference.

## What is real versus modeled?
The four downtown cells and their current thermal values are provider-backed. Vulnerability and exposure are derived planning metrics. Intervention effects and TEC are modeled. CP-SAT selections are optimized planning outputs. Agent decisions are governed. Gemma is explanatory.

## Can it scale beyond four cells?
Yes. The platform uses PostGIS, spatial APIs, tiled provider AOIs, and graph-based routing. Scaling requires onboarding and validating additional provider coverage; it does not mean inventing values between deployments.

## Privacy and cost?
Current analysis is cell/aggregate based, not individual tracking. Local inference reduces data egress. Costs are bounded through scheduled provider ingestion, cached OSM context, deterministic engines, and one appropriately sized local model.

## Robustness and uncertainty?
Failures are explicit, confidence is retained, quality snapshots require review, provider provenance is preserved, and the LLM has a semantic firewall plus truthful fallback state. Modeled effects must be sensitivity-tested and field-validated.

## Causal limitations?
Counterfactual temperature deltas are planning assumptions. Attribution is diagnostic, not causal proof. A deployment should use pilots, monitoring, and causal evaluation before claiming realized cooling.

## Public-sector and disaster relevance?
The audit trail, local AI, human gate, budget feasibility, accessibility analysis, and neutral unobserved regions support defensible preparedness planning. During extreme heat, the same architecture can prioritize outreach, safe routes, and facilities using freshly validated provider data.
